What is MyPrep?

MyPrep is a student-built study workspace designed to organise preparation charts, chapter progress and exam-specific study material in one place.

Development

MyPrep has been developed iteratively: the account system and saved progress came first, followed by the Prep Chart workspace, exam-specific chart data, Prepo, filters, display modes and study management.

Sources & data

Physics formulas used by Prepo are based on the uploaded Physics Handbook. The JEE Main chapter list is based on the syllabus supplied for this project. Exam-alignment and chapter metadata are kept separate from account-specific progress.
